# 📊 Olist Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **E-commerce Sales & Delivery Analytics Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to analyze revenue, orders, products, customer satisfaction, and delivery performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project transforms Olist e-commerce data into an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides a clear view of business performance.

The dashboard focuses on:

- Revenue and order performance
- Product category performance
- Customer satisfaction
- Delivery performance
- Payment method insights
- Monthly revenue trends

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

The Overview dashboard provides a high-level summary of the business through key KPIs and visualizations.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Revenue: $13.59M
- Total Orders: 99K
- AOV: $136.68
- Total Customers: 96K
- On-Time Delivery: 91.89%
- Average Review Score: 4.09

**Visualizations:**
- Revenue Trend Over Time
- Customer Satisfaction by Review Score
- Revenue by Top Categories
- Order Status Distribution
- Delivery Performance

### 2. Sales & Products

This dashboard focuses on revenue, product categories, and payment insights.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Revenue: $13.59M
- Total Orders: 99K
- Products Sold: 113K
- Unique Products: 33K

**Visualizations:**
- Revenue by Top Categories
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue Share by Product Category
- Average Order Value by Payment Type

## 📈 Key Insights

- Total revenue analyzed is approximately **$13.59M**.
- The dashboard records approximately **99K orders**.
- The average order value is approximately **$136.68**.
- Approximately **91.89%** of orders were delivered on time.
- The average review score is **4.09**.
- **Health & Beauty** is the highest-revenue category among the displayed top categories.
- Review score **5** represents the largest share of customer reviews.
